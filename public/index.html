ABOUT US

Connecting Africa's Healthcare - One Record at a Time

Healthclouda is a secure, cloud-based Electronic Health Record (EHR) and student data platform built for African universities. We reduce queues, replace paper records, and enable secure record-sharing within campus health systems. By leveraging Microsoft Azure Healthcare Cloud Services, we provide reliability, performance, and compliance while tailoring solutions to the unique realities of African students and clinics.

Our Mission

To empower university clinics and students through instant, reliable, and secure access to health records, reducing administrative bottlenecks and enabling healthier student communities.

Our Vision

A future where every African student’s health record is available in the cloud, easily accessible at their campus clinic, and securely shared between departments when needed. By eliminating paper files and long queues, HealthClouda-Campus supports faster care and stronger student well-being.

Problem We Solve

In many African universities, student health records are still stored in paper files, locked away in clinic offices. Students often face repeated tests, long queues, and delays because their records are inaccessible. This results in wasted time, unnecessary costs, and delayed treatment.

Our Solution

Built on Microsoft Azure Healthcare Cloud Services, HealthClouda-Campus delivers high performance, reliability, and compliance with global healthcare standards. With a unique HealthClouda Student ID, students’ records are securely accessible across their university clinic, ensuring continuity of care.
