One Platform, Every Healthcare Role Covered

Our secure, user-friendly Electronic Health Record system is purpose-built for hospitals and health facilities looking to simplify documentation, enhance collaboration, and improve patient care.

Effortless Patient Workflows

Every clinic operates differently and HealthClouda adapts. You’ll get smart, customizable clinical forms that reflect your exact workflow: registration, history taking, vitals, diagnostic notes and follow ups, all shaped around how your team works. There is no forcing patients or staff into rigid processes. That means faster onboarding for new staff, fewer input errors and more time to focus on delivering care rather than clicking through screens.

Proactive Intelligence that Keeps Patients Safe

HealthClouda includes safety alerts for allergies, drug interactions, critical vitals, duplicate records, and follow-up reminders. When something looks off, the system prompts staff to double-check and intervene early, helping ensure students receive the right care at the right time.

Built for Africa's Realities

HealthClouda does not just work in Africa, it is built for it. Clinics benefit from a full offline mode so work continues even during power or connectivity drops. The platform is cost effective for varying budgets and scales gracefully whether you are a single practitioner clinic or a growing hospital. Intuitive designs and optional local language support help everyone on your team, from receptionists to clinicians, adopt the system quickly.

WHO WE ARE

Healthclouda is a secure, cloud-based Electronic Health Record (EHR) and patient data platform designed to reduce long hospital queues and enable seamless, secure sharing of medical records between healthcare facilities in Nigeria and Sub-Saharan Africa. It is built to serve hospitals, clinics, schools with medical staff, and pharmacies. The system is built on Microsoft Azure Healthcare Cloud Services for performance, reliability, and compliance.

Our Mission

To empower healthcare providers and patients through instant, reliable, and secure access to medical records, improving efficiency and saving lives

Our Vision

A  cloud-based EHR platform built for seamless hospital-to-hospital record sharing in Nigeria/Africa, solving long queues by improving data retrieval.

Problem We Solve

Built on Microsoft Azure Healthcare Cloud Services, HealthClouda offers high performance, reliability, and compliance with global healthcare data standards. With a unique HealthClouda ID, patient records are securely accessible from any registered healthcare facility instantly.

Our Solution

Built on Microsoft Azure Healthcare Cloud Services, HealthClouda-Campus delivers high performance, reliability, and compliance with global healthcare standards. With a unique HealthClouda Student ID, students’ records are securely accessible across their university clinic, ensuring continuity of care.
