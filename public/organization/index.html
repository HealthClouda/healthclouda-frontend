Your Wellbeing Matters...

Here's a quick dose of wellness inspiration to keep your mind and body in sync throughout the semester.

Drink More Water

Your brain runs on water. Keep a reusable bottle with you and aim for 8 glasses daily.

Mental Health is Health

Listen to your emotions. If you're constantly tired, anxious, or demotivated, reach out — early support is confidential and free.

Wash & Protect

Frequent handwashing reduces infections. Carry sanitizer, avoid sharing cups, and mask up if you feel unwell.

Eat Real Food

Good grades need good fuel. Choose whole foods over processed snacks; your body and brain will thank you.

Morning Light Matters

10 minutes of morning sunlight boosts your energy and focus. It helps your body produce serotonin for a happier mood.

Rest Well

Quality rest helps your immune system and focus. Aim for 7–8 hours and let your mind reset — rest is part of progress.
